# Payday: Spec

**Rank in portfolio:** #10 of 15 (easiest → hardest) · **Difficulty:** Easy

## One-liner

Payday countdown, bills calendar and how much is left.

## Problem / audience

- **Who it's for:** Monthly-paid workers and people with multiple income streams.
- **The problem it solves:** Hard to see bills, income dates and leftover cash in one place.

## MVP features (ship with only these)

1. Payday countdown
2. Income calendar (multiple incomes)
3. Upcoming bills list
4. Estimated leftover cash

## Not in MVP

- Forecasting
- Recurring payments
- Financial milestones

## Monetisation

| Model | Detail |
|---|---|
| Free | Countdown + bills, with ads |
| Paid | £2.99/month: forecasting + multiple incomes |
| Ads | Light |

Prices are starting points from the portfolio brief; test before committing.

## Platform & stack

- **Platform:** Expo.
- **Stack:** shared portfolio foundation (Expo, PostHog, RevenueCat, AdMob). See the portfolio roadmap.
- **Data:** Device only.

## Growth engine hooks

- **Shareable output:** Payday countdown cards.
- **Content pillars:** Payday money habits; Bills planning
